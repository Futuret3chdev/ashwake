# ASHWAKE

Public beta by **futuret3ch**. Pilot the traction city **VANTAGE** across a living outland. Eat towns for **t3x**, or let the wild cool the world before it eats you.

## Play

- **W / S** drive, **A** turns left, **D** turns right
- **Space** jaw, **Shift** overdrive, **Q** aegis, **E** mercy
- On a phone, use the stick and the four skill buttons
- Dock from pause to bank t3x. If the city falls, a third is kept
- Spend t3x at the Stack. Progress stays on this device

Multiplayer sorties come after beta.
